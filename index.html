❓ FAQ & Live Help

Got a Question?

Browse common questions below — or ask our chatbot instantly. For anything else, WhatsApp us!

Zoom ya WhatsApp — kaunsa use hoga? ▼
Dono available hain! Zoom video class ke liye aur WhatsApp video call zyada asaan sessions ke liye. Aap jo prefer karein woh choose karein — dono pe Quran teaching excellent kaam karti hai.
Kis umar ke bachon ko parha sakte hain? ▼
4 saal se lekar 70+ tak! Chhotay bachon ke liye Hafiza Maizam Gulfraz hain, aur adults ke liye alag specialist teachers. Quran seekhna kabhi bhi shuru ho sakta hai!
Kya sisters ke liye female teacher milegi? ▼
Bilkul! Hafiza Maizam Gulfraz aur Molana Gulbahar Shabbir — dono qualified Hafizah hain aur exclusively sisters aur bachiyaan parhaati hain. Enquiry mein zaroor mention karein.
UK/USA/Europe ke time zone mein class kaise hogi? ▼
Hamare teachers Pakistan (PKT) mein hain — subah 6 baje se raat 11 baje tak available hain. UK evening = Pakistan late night, US morning = Pakistan afternoon. Aapke time zone ke hisaab se fixed slot miley ga.
Trial class bilkul free hai? ▼
Haan — 100% free! Koi payment nahi, koi commitment nahi. 30 minute ki trial class mein teacher se milo, apna level assess karo, aur phir decide karo. Koi pressure nahi.
Class miss ho jaye to? ▼
Koi problem nahi — 12 ghante pehle WhatsApp karein aur class reschedule ho jaye gi. Emergency mein bhi samajh ke kaam karte hain. Aapki suvidha hamari priority hai.
Hifz mein kitna time lagta hai? ▼
Bacche ke liye (regular daily classes): 2–4 saal. Adult learner ke liye: 4–7 saal. Daily participation aur revision se time kam ho sakta hai. Hafiz Ahsan Sammer ke students ne 5 Juz ek saal mein hifz kiye hain!
Payment kaise hogi? ▼
Monthly payment — bank transfer, Western Union, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, ya aapke mulk ki currency mein. WhatsApp pe contact karein aur hum aapko sahi option batayein ge.
