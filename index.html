❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What platform do you use for classes? ▼
We use Zoom for video classes and WhatsApp video call for more informal/flexible sessions. You can choose whichever you prefer. Both work excellently for Quran teaching.
What age groups do you teach? ▼
We teach students of all ages — from children as young as 4 years old to adults in their 60s. We have dedicated children's teachers and adult specialists. It is never too late to learn the Quran!
Do you offer female teachers for sisters? ▼
Yes, absolutely. We have qualified female teachers (Hafizah with Ijazah) available for sisters and for children who prefer a female teacher. Please specify this when you enquire and we will match you accordingly.
How do timings work with different time zones? ▼
Our teachers are based in Pakistan (PKT timezone) and are available from 6am to 11pm PKT, which covers mornings in Europe/UK, evenings in North America, and afternoons in the Gulf. We will agree a fixed weekly slot that suits you.
Is the free trial really free? ▼
Yes, 100% free — no credit card, no payment, no obligation. You get one 30-minute trial class to meet your teacher and assess your level. After that, you decide if you want to continue.
What if I miss a class? ▼
Life happens! If you give us at least 12 hours notice, we will reschedule the class at no extra charge. Emergency absences are handled on a case-by-case basis with full understanding.
Which Tajweed rules / Qira'ah do you teach? ▼
We teach the Hafs an Asim recitation (the most widely used in Pakistan, UK and most of the world). All Tajweed rules are taught according to the standard classical manuals (Al-Jazariyyah, Tuhfat ul Atfaal).
